How this applies the thesis

Relationships before isolated features

The engine derives requirements from your scene: a changed return needs a reference, accompanying elements need a shared opportunity, and an action’s presented consequence needs to be encountered.

Numbers with a specific meaning

The index describes coverage of these relationships in the situations you supply. Timing can refine it to a share of the described interval. It does not measure emotion, notice or the amount of time a scene needs to be effective.

Your meaning stays yours

The words you enter label the scene; the tool does not interpret free text. Its rules use the concrete selections and timings. Contrast, ambiguity and optional discovery can be intentional. You decide whether a suggested comparison serves your reading.

An author-designed application of Yiwei Ye’s Atmospheric Communication Model. Numerical conventions demonstrate one bounded use of the model; they are not empirical calibration or proof of player response. Runs offline without an account or AI service.
